# Bank-Customer-Transaction-Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing bank customer and transaction data using Power BI and SQL.

The main goal was to understand the bank's performance, customer activity, transaction behavior, and payment channel usage. The dashboard presents key insights in one place to help business teams monitor performance and make data-driven decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall bank performance and customer activity.
- Identify revenue trends across cities and banking categories.
- Understand customer preferences for banking products and payment channels.
- Support data-driven decisions for customer targeting and marketing campaigns.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard highlights key KPIs, including total customers, active and inactive customers, and average daily transactions. It visualizes revenue by city and category, customer distribution by product and gender, transaction types, payment channel usage, and marketing campaign channels.

These insights help business teams understand customer behavior, identify trends, and make informed decisions.

##  Key Insights

- The dashboard provides an overview of active and inactive customers.
- Revenue can be compared across cities and categories to identify stronger-performing areas.
- Customer distribution across banking products helps teams understand product usage.
- Payment channel analysis highlights how customers interact with banking services.
- Customer segments and marketing channel comparisons can support more targeted campaigns.


##  Key Takeaway

This project demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be used to transform banking data into meaningful business insights. The dashboard helps teams monitor customer activity, analyze transactions, understand channel usage, and support data-driven decisions.
